# Establish product direction

Read this workflow only when Product Vision or a usable Product Goal is missing, or when the current Product Goal has been achieved and the next Goal is needed.

1. Start a dialogue in the user's preferred language.
2. When Product Vision is missing or unusable, load `$product-vision-board`. Briefly explain its simple and extended variants, let the human choose, and follow that skill's field-by-field dialogue. Do not substitute a generic vision statement for the completed board.
3. Keep the creative product direction with the human. Summarize, connect, challenge, and expose assumptions without inventing strategic intent or treating human confirmation as market validation.
4. Draft and confirm the Product Vision Board at `product-backlog/product-vision.md` before establishing the Product Goal.
5. Use the confirmed board as input to collaborate on one concrete current Product Goal at `product-backlog/current-product-goal.md`. The goal must advance the Product Vision and help verify one or more important board hypotheses while remaining an outcome rather than a technical solution.
6. Ask one to three open questions at a time during Product Goal discovery and ask for explicit human confirmation before the first affected Sprint Planning.
7. Mark each artifact as human-verified only after the human confirms its complete current content. Remove prior human verification after a meaningful content change and request confirmation again.

Maintain one current Product Goal. Fulfill or explicitly abandon it before activating another.
